Melo

Give every app its own volume, its own output device, and its own sound — from the menu bar.

Download Melo __VERSION__

Requires macOS 15.4 or later · Apple silicon · Free

First launch

  1. Unzip the download and drag Melo to your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify the developer — that is expected, and it appears for every app not distributed through Apple's paid program.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Melo.

You only do this once. After that Melo opens normally and updates itself. If you prefer the Terminal, xattr -cr /Applications/Melo.app does the same thing in one step.

What it does

  • Per-app volume. Turn down a browser tab without touching your music.
  • Per-app output. Send one app to your headphones and another to your speakers.
  • Sound shaping. An equalizer, headphone profiles, and Smart Sound that adapts as you listen.
  • Scenes. Save a whole setup and bring it back in one click.
  • Stays out of the way. Lives in the menu bar, follows your volume keys.
